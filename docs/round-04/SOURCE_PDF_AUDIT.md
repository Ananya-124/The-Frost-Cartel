### 2. THE FIVE AUTHORITATIVE ROUTES & SEQUENCES

All five qualified teams execute a unique, non-overlapping sequence across the campus floors, converging on the 7th Floor for the shared finale:

* **Route 1:** Fourth Floor → Second Floor → Fifth Floor → Third Floor → Seventh Floor (`4 → 2 → 5 → 3 → 7`)
* **Route 2:** Second Floor → Fourth Floor → Sixth Floor → Third Floor → Seventh Floor (`2 → 4 → 6 → 3 → 7`)
* **Route 3:** Second Floor → Sixth Floor → Third Floor → Fifth Floor → Seventh Floor (`2 → 6 → 3 → 5 → 7`)
* **Route 4:** Fourth Floor → Sixth Floor → Second Floor → Fifth Floor → Seventh Floor (`4 → 6 → 2 → 5 → 7`)
* **Route 5:** Sixth Floor → Second Floor → Fifth Floor → Fourth Floor → Seventh Floor (`6 → 2 → 5 → 4 → 7`)
